Sunrise, sunset, golden hour and moon times for any place

Sky Almanac shows the day's sunrise and sunset, golden hour and blue hour, moonrise, moonset and moon phase for any place on Earth, with the sky's colour across the whole day and where to look for the sun and moon.

  • For photographers: golden and blue hours, the direction the light comes from, and full moons rising at sunset.
  • For stargazers: tonight's dark-sky window, when the sun is 18° below the horizon and the moon is down.
  • Coming up: the next full moon and new moon, supermoons, solstices, equinoxes, clock changes and the year's earliest sunset.
  • Add to calendar in one click, with a reminder before the light is best.

Free, with no sign-up. The sun and moon are calculated in your browser by SunCalcNet, an open-source .NET library; weather forecasts come from Open-Meteo.
